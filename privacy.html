Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

Soyogi ("the app") is designed to keep your data on your own devices. We do not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data the app stores

  • Meditation sessions (start time, duration, selected sound, and — when recorded on Apple Watch — average, minimum and maximum heart rate) are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Sessions recorded on Apple Watch are sent directly to your own paired iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity. They are not sent to any server.
  • Settings (duration, sound, volume) are stored on your device.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

  • With your permission, the Apple Watch app reads your heart rate during a session to show it and to keep a summary in your history.
  • With your permission, the app saves your meditation time to Mindful Minutes in the Health app.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no servers, no analytics or tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • Notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device to tell you a session has ended.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Mindful Minutes saved to Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

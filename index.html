One tap. Real rain.

Soyogi is a quiet meditation timer. Press once, and real recordings of rain, forest or ocean begin — and your time is logged.

  • Real field recordings of rain, forest and ocean waves
  • Choose 1–60 minutes, or no limit
  • A calendar that slowly fills with green as you practice
  • Apple Watch: start on your wrist, with heart rate, even without your iPhone
  • Free. No ads, no account, no subscriptions. Works offline.

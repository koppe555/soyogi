FAQ

The sound stops when I lock my iPhone in a Silent session.

Without sound, iOS may suspend the app while the screen is locked. You'll get a notification when the time is up, and the planned time is saved when you open the app.

Where does the nature sound play on Apple Watch?

Through connected headphones such as AirPods, or the watch speaker. Turn the Digital Crown during a session to change the volume.

Heart rate isn't shown on Apple Watch.

Allow Soyogi to read heart rate in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices on your iPhone, or in the Health settings on your watch.

Does Soyogi back up my history?

Your history stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. It is included in your device backup, but Soyogi has no servers or accounts.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Mindful Minutes saved to Health can be deleted in the Health app.
